Food delivery operations dashboard

  • Tracks platform health for a food delivery service: demand, customer retention, SLA performance, and restaurant-level operational bottlenecks.
  • Stakeholder-focused views for executives, CX teams, and operations, each filterable to full-year 2024 or any quarter.
  • Time intelligence and P95 latency metrics, with targeted charts for root-cause analysis of delays.
  • Key finding: demand held steady across 2024 while ratings and on-time delivery slipped. Q4 delays traced back to prep time at a few high-impact restaurants, which drove most SLA misses even as the delivery tail improved.

Interactive Use the page tabs along the bottom of the report to move between views.

What it answers

  • Is demand stable, or are orders and revenue shifting over the year?
  • How are customer ratings and SLA attainment trending?
  • Which cuisines and restaurants contribute most to revenue?
  • How strong is quarter-over-quarter retention, and how many customers order only once?
  • When does demand peak by hour of day and day of week?
  • Which restaurants drive Q4 delays, and is prep time or delivery time the cause?

How it's structured

  • Executive overview: orders, revenue, average order value, ratings, SLA 60m attainment, P95 fulfillment
  • Customer behavior: retention, order frequency, reorder timing, day-hour demand patterns
  • Q4 operations deep dive: prep and delivery trends, late-order delay buckets, restaurant-level contributors

About the data

  • Synthetic transactional dataset, expanded from a public Kaggle food-ordering dataset
  • Simulates lunch and dinner peaks, weekend surges, reorders, restaurant prep differences, and worsening Q4 delays
  • Sample data and a data dictionary are in the repo
